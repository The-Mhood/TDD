## 3. The eight chapters in the Ọmà Ala

The eight-chapter structure (`WORLD_ARCHITECTURE.md`: World → Chapter → Environment → Locations → Scenes) is placed in the coherent geography. Each chapter maps to a geographical domain of the Ọmà Ala.

## 4. ST05–ST07 integration (CH01, Ahanwụ)

Per `ST05_ST07_APPROVAL_RECORD.md` (APPROVED, owner-supplied): these are **ordinary** environmental/community states — no ritual/shrine/masquerade without an established story requirement and cultural verification.

| State | Definition | World integration (Ahanwụ) |
|---|---|---|
| **ST05 — Rain** | Home Settlement in rainy conditions | Ahanwụ in rainy conditions: wet `ama` square, muddy paths, compound life under rain; driven by the world **weather system** (rainy-season rainfall). Weather/wet-surface only — **no ritual** |
| **ST06 — Market day** | Crowded village square, ordinary trading & goods | Ahanwụ's **market-day surge** (Afo) — traders, stalls, goods, crowds; a market system instance, **not** an isolated crowd. Ordinary trading, no sacred vocabulary |
| **ST07 — Gathering** | Village square prepared for a community assembly | Ahanwụ's `ama` square prepared for a **community assembly** — ordinary civic/social gathering, **not** a religious ceremony. Part of the village's public-life population |

**Integration requirement:** ST05/ST06/ST07 are **not isolated contradictory scenes.** They are the Ahanwụ village square populated by the **same world population system** and driven by the **same weather/calendar systems**:
- ST05's rain is the world weather system applied to Ahanwụ (and it also affects the market/farmland/river).
- ST06's market crowd is the world population surge on Ahanwụ's market day.
- ST07's gathering is the world population assembled at the `ama` square.
The named actors and crowd in these states come from the Ahanwụ persistent population + event surge, not from separate hand-placed props.

## 6. Location & scene reconciliation (method)

This register **reconciles the entire game** against the world. The method:

1. **Register every chapter/scene/location** from the Drive `LOCATION_MASTER_REGISTER.md` v2.9 / `SCENE_AND_MICROLOCATION_MASTER_REGISTER.md` v1.9 (8 environments, 142 locations, 46 interiors, 135 scenes, 40 environment-state refs; CH01 = 46 scenes/68 entries).
2. **Each scene gets:** geographic placement (settlement/domain), access (route/water), surroundings (farm/forest/river), nearby people (which NPC categories), NPCs present, **use outside the scene** (is this location also ordinary-world activity?), and **ordinary-day vs market/festival/event changes** (how it differs when a market/festival/event is active).
3. **World connection:** every scene is connected to the world system it draws from (population, calendar, weather, economy).

**Scene-category population budget:** the Drive register's `Scene Category` column (AMBIENT NPC SCENE / WORLD ACTIVITY SCENE / ATMOSPHERIC SCENE / BACKGROUND ENVIRONMENT) is the **authoritative population budget per scene**, authored by design — the number of NPCs/props that scene requires, drawn from the settlement's population tiers.

**Deliverable:** Design produces the **complete scene-to-world reconciliation table** (every 135 scenes: placement, access, surroundings, nearby people, NPCs, outside-use, ordinary-vs-event changes, world connection). This register fixes the canonical frame; Design maps each scene's world coordinates. **Contradictions are flagged to PM/Story/owner — never silently changed.**

## 7. Physical / remembered / concealed / supernatural / symbolic — distinct locations

The story uses five kinds of location. They are kept **distinct** in world space and narrative status:

| Kind | Examples | World treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **Physical** | Ahanwụ, the Ọmà, farmland, market towns, craft quarters | Ordinary-world locations; full population/weather/calendar |
| **Remembered** | Nneka's memory scenes (seven memories, Ch.1–7) | Memory spaces; narrative; **not** ordinary-world population |
| **Concealed** | Hidden/truth locations (Nneka truth, concealment of memories) | Narrative; revealed through the story |
| **Supernatural** | Ancestral Realm, Idemili's river domain, Amadioha's thunder, Okwu | Narrative spaces; **Ancestral Realm retains established narrative status** — exploratory designs do not silently become canon |
| **Symbolic** | The boundary place, the Hollow King's work | Narrative/symbolic geography |

**Ancestral Realm status (protected):** it is a distinct narrative space, not an ordinary-world location. Any world-space representation is a **Story decision** with the established narrative status preserved; exploratory designs must not silently promote to canon.
