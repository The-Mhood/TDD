# Igbo Name Catalogue — Research Deliverable

**Document ID:** TDD-IMPL-NAMES-001 · **Version:** 1.0 (Research DRAFT) · **Date:** 2026-11-21
**Author:** Research (drafted by PM/agent for verification). **Status:** DRAFT — **requires specialist verification before finalisation.**
**Purpose:** Supply a substantial catalogue of historically and culturally grounded Igbo names with supporting evidence. **Research supplies names and evidence; Research does NOT assign names to individual characters** (PM allocates, Story confirms narrative fit).

## How to read this catalogue
Each entry carries eight fields: (1) spelling/diacritics · (2) meaning/interpretation · (3) historical/geographic/linguistic origin · (4) cultural relevance & original context · (5) pronunciation where reliably documented · (6) traceable sources · (7) confidence level & disputed interpretations · (8) cultural-sensitivity warnings.

**Confidence key:** HIGH = well-documented in Igbo language/tradition. MEDIUM = partially documented or regionally variable. LOW = meaning uncertain / coined / disputed; requires verification.

**Standing rule:** Do **not** invent meanings or present uncertain folklore as established history. Distinguish **traditional/documented meanings** from **modern, Christian or colonial reinterpretations**. Entries marked LOW/MEDIUM are flagged for specialist verification — they are candidates, not settled fact.

> **Important:** This is a Research *draft*. The Igbo-speaking world is diverse; meanings and attributions vary by dialect, region and community. Final sourcing should be verified against traceable references (e.g. standard Igbo dictionaries, scholarly works on Igbo religion and naming, documented oral traditions, and recognised Igbo literary sources). Cultural-sensitivity items route through CD-2.

### Adanna
1. **Spelling:** Adanna
2. **Meaning:** Uncertain / possibly coined. The element *nnа* (father) exists, but the full name's traditional meaning is **disputed** — do not assign a settled meaning.
3. **Origin:** Uncertain; may be a coined protagonist name.
4. **Cultural relevance:** Fictional protagonist; not a documented historical/folklore figure.
5. **Pronunciation:** a-DAH-nah
6. **Sources:** Game register (`CHARACTER_DESIGN_REGISTER.md`); no established external etymology.
7. **Confidence:** LOW (meaning) — flag for Research/consultant verification before any meaning is stated.
8. **Sensitivity:** None inherent; do not attach a fabricated meaning to justify the name.

### Amadioha
1. **Spelling:** Amadioha (also Amodioha, Amadioha)
2. **Meaning:** Thunder/lightning deity; associated with **judgment and justice**.
3. **Origin:** Igbo traditional religion; thunder god found across Igbo communities.
4. **Cultural relevance:** A **documented** Igbo deity of thunder, judgment and destruction; colour red; symbol the **white ram**. The game's Ch.5 figure (human form, consultant-approved per OAD-05) is a fictionalised treatment of this documented deity.
5. **Pronunciation:** a-mah-DYO-ha
6. **Sources:** Documented Igbo religion; corroborated by the game's own register ("thunder and lightning deity associated with justice; colour red; symbol a white ram").
7. **Confidence:** HIGH (documented deity).
8. **Sensitivity:** **HIGH** — a real deity. The game's visual design is explicitly fiction (per the register); any depiction must not misrepresent the deity's role or invent doctrine. Consultant approval already recorded for the human form (OAD-05).

### Ezeani
1. **Spelling:** Ezeani
2. **Meaning:** Root element *Eze* = "king/chief". Full-name meaning **uncertain/disputed** — possibly "king of the land/earth"; do not settle.
3. **Origin:** Igbo; *Eze* is a documented title/root.
4. **Cultural relevance:** Fictional character (Adanna's father, warrior-king). The *Eze* element aligns with a real Igbo title root.
5. **Pronunciation:** eh-ZEH-ah-nee
6. **Sources:** Game register; *Eze* as a title root is documented Igbo.
7. **Confidence:** MEDIUM (root documented; full-name meaning LOW/uncertain).
8. **Sensitivity:** Moderate — *Eze* is a real title; avoid implying a specific historical kingship claim.

### Nneka
1. **Spelling:** Nneka
2. **Meaning:** "**Mother is supreme / mother is great**" (*Nne* = mother; *ka* = supreme/great). A documented, meaningful Igbo name.
3. **Origin:** Igbo; widely used female name.
4. **Cultural relevance:** Fits the character (Adanna's mother) precisely; the traditional meaning reinforces the narrative.
5. **Pronunciation:** NNEH-kah
6. **Sources:** Documented Igbo name/etymology; game register.
7. **Confidence:** HIGH (documented meaning).
8. **Sensitivity:** None inherent.

### Okwu
1. **Spelling:** Okwu
2. **Meaning:** "**word / speech / matter / case**" — a real Igbo word.
3. **Origin:** Igbo; a common word and name root.
4. **Cultural relevance:** Used for the spirit companion/guide; the "word/speech" meaning is thematically apt for a guide figure.
5. **Pronunciation:** OK-woo
6. **Sources:** Documented Igbo vocabulary; game register.
7. **Confidence:** HIGH (documented word/name).
8. **Sensitivity:** None inherent; note the character is "entirely fictional" and kept distinct from real masquerade forms (OAD-04).

### Idemili
1. **Spelling:** Idemili
2. **Meaning:** River deity/goddess (associated with the river domain; python as sacred animal).
3. **Origin:** Igbo traditional religion; river deity.
4. **Cultural relevance:** A **documented** Igbo river deity; the game's Ch.4 figure (python sacred animal) is a fictionalised treatment. The register states "Documented: river deity; python is her sacred animal. Appearance is fiction."
5. **Pronunciation:** ee-DEh-MEE-lee
6. **Sources:** Documented Igbo religion; game register.
7. **Confidence:** HIGH (documented deity; regional variants exist).
8. **Sensitivity:** **HIGH** — a real deity; fictionalised appearance, consultant-sensitive. Python is sacred to this deity and is never an enemy/loot (OAD-14).

### The Hollow King
1. **Spelling:** The Hollow King (title, not an Igbo personal name)
2. **Meaning:** Descriptive fictional title; no Igbo etymology.
3. **Origin:** Fictional antagonist (Ch.7); "entirely fictional" per the register.
4. **Cultural relevance:** Fictional; not a documented figure.
5. **Pronunciation:** n/a
6. **Sources:** Game register.
7. **Confidence:** N/A (fictional title).
8. **Sensitivity:** None inherent; ensure the "king of the dead" framing does not read as a claim about real Igbo funerary or ancestral practice (CD-2).

## 6. Cultural-sensitivity warnings (standing)
- **Deities (Amadioha, Idemili, Ala, Olokun, etc.):** real figures of Igbo traditional religion. Any depiction is a fictionalised treatment; route through cultural review (CD-2). Do not misrepresent roles or invent doctrine.
- **Titles (Eze, Igwe, Ozo, Nze):** real titles. Avoid implying specific historical or living title-holders.
- **Masquerade / raffia spirit imagery:** restricted and culturally significant (OAD-04). Okwu must stay distinct from real masquerade forms.
- **Uli motifs:** documented women's art form; do not reproduce named motifs without consultant input (OAD-06).
- **Ancestral / funerary content:** do not present as real Igbo practice without sign-off (CD-2, Ch.3/7 constraints).
- **Distinguish traditional meanings from modern/Christian/colonial reinterpretations.** Some names acquire new meanings under Christian or colonial use; do not treat those as the traditional meaning.
