## Deliverable 2 — Complete named-character roster reconciled against the storyline
The roster reconciles cleanly with the established narrative:
- **Adanna–Ezeani–Nneka** core: Adanna (daughter of Ezeani), Nneka (mother, killed by Ezeani), Ezeani (father/final boss). The established relationship (OAD-34: Nneka offered herself; Ezeani killed her at her request and concealed the truth; Adanna was six) **remains intact** — no name change affects it.
- **Amadioha (Ch.5), Idemili (Ch.4)**: documented deities, fictionalised — names retained.
- **The Hollow King (Ch.7)**: fictional antagonist — title retained.
- **Okwu**: fictional spirit guide — name retained (meaning "word/speech" is thematically apt).
- **Supporting cast**: 13 roles with approved names, distributed across the home settlement and palace/rebel factions.

**Reconciliation result:** 20 named characters (7 main + 13 supporting). No established name contradicts the storyline.

- **Enemy taxonomy** (separate workstream, `ENEMY_DECISION_PACKAGE.md`): the rejected Maskbearer needs an ordinary-physical-enemy replacement name (Story/PM to propose; owner to approve). This is an enemy, not a named character.
- **Memory-scene younger versions** of Adanna/Ezeani are the same characters, not new names.

## Deliverable 4 — Existing names proposed for change, with reasons
**PM proposes NO changes to established character names.** Per the standing rule, an established character is not renamed merely because another name sounds more interesting. Specifically:
- **Retain all 7 main names** and all 13 supporting names.
- **Flagged for Research verification (not rename):** the meanings of **Adanna** and **Ezeani** are uncertain (LOW confidence, see `IGBO_NAME_CATALOGUE.md`). PM recommends Research verify the etymology and document it, but **not** rename — both names are narratively embedded and changing them would disrupt dialogue, scripts and story references without narrative justification.
- **Flagged for consultant verification (not rename):** the 9 supporting names not yet source-checked (OAD-15). Verification of meaning/sensitivity, not rename, unless verification reveals a genuine cultural problem — in which case PM documents the reason and effect on Drive folders, dialogue, scripts and story refs, and routes to owner.

## Deliverable 5 — Recommended named-character target for the full game
**Target: 20 named characters (7 main + 13 supporting).** PM recommends **holding at 20** — a coherent, non-inflated cast. Additions only for a **genuine narrative/gameplay gap** identified in a chapter, drawn from the Research candidate pool (`IGBO_NAME_CATALOGUE.md` §5), with Story narrative-fit confirmation and owner approval. **Do not inflate the cast to use every name Research discovers.**
