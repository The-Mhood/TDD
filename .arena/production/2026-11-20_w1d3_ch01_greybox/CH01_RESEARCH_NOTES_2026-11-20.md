## Verified claims (sources below agree)
1. Built form is **mud (laterite/aja ụ́pa) walls** + **thatched roofs**; red-earth construction dominant in precolonial Igboland. [S1, S2, S4]
2. **High-pitched** roofs (shed heavy rain); thatch tied over bamboo/timber pole framework; rafia or grass thatch species (Ejo/Eyo, Ata grass; ngwo palm fronds; Raphia leaflets). [S2, S4]
3. **Compound (ezina ụlo / ezi okwu style):** cluster of huts around a courtyard (**mbara ezi**), enclosed by a wall (**ngwulu**, ≈1.5–2.5 m) with a single **gate (ama)**; Obi (family head's house) faces the gate across the forecourt. [S2, S3, S4, S5]
4. **Obi** = patriarch's hall/ancestral reception; positioned to face main entrance; scale/detail rise with status (titled men/Ọzọ → more elaborate). [S2, S3, S5]
5. Women's houses (**ṃkpuke**) vs men's (**obi**); count of ṃkpuke indicates wives; obi marking social class; ruler's obi = epitome structure. [S1]
6. Decorative systems: **uli** wall motifs (ephemeral, dark pigment, applied by women); carved pillars for titled men (leopard/tortoise/yam tendrils); paintings on walls by owner's taste/purpose. [S1, S3]
7. **M̀barị̀** = religious/communal architectural form (shrine-like, strong walls, long runway/gate) — SACRED class; do not depict casually or invent forms. Out of scope for CH01 fiction. [S1]
8. Materials sourced locally: mud, timber, bamboo, thatch, clay pots; practical tropical climate response (cool interiors, moisture-shedding thatch). [S1, S2, S4, S5]
9. Achebe's *Things Fall Apart* corroborates: red-earth thick compound wall, single gate, obi immediately behind gate, wives' huts in half-moon, barn against wall. [S1]
