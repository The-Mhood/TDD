## Verified claims (multi-source agreement)
1. Mud (laterite / compacted mud with straw, wattle-and-daub OR mass-mud adobe) walls; reddish-brown. [R1, R2, R3, R4, R5]
2. Thick walls (thermal mass); broad broad foundations ≈0.4–0.6 m (R1); raised threshold protects from rain/flood (R3).
3. **High-pitched roof**; pitched/gable/hip forms; thatch (aju / atani / ngwo; ata = Imperata cylindrica bundles) over bamboo/rafia-pole framework; raffia rope (ekekre) lashing; four triangular corner stakes hold roof frame; wide eaves overhang; eaves ventilation gap (R1, R2, R4, R5). [R1, R2, R4, R5]
4. Framework sometimes built as paired roof frames (triangular trusses) before lifting (R4).
5. Wattle-and-daub: bamboo framework + horizontal members + rope; door/window frames installed during wall construction (R4).
6. Rectangular OR circular plans both occur; rectangular common for main houses, circular/oval for some hut types (R2, R4). **CH01 uses rectangular-with-hipped-roof as engineering assumption (matches greybox HUT_A/B/C) — density/plan mix remains CD's call.**
7. Small doorway; window frames rare/absent on family units (R4 notes frames installed during construction, implying openings exist but small).
8. Interior: raised sleeping platform, mats, central hearth, storage pots + baskets; indigo cloth for doorway drape (broadly West African; fiction-safe as plain cloth). [R1/R3 adjacent + master-palette note] (moderately sourced — flagged)
