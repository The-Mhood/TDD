Documented vs interpretation (kept distinct, general level only):
- *Documented, general:* precolonial Igbo everyday dress centred on woven cloth wrappers worn at the waist or chest, with cloth type/dye signalling means and occasion; indigo dyeing and undyed cloth are broadly attested in the region; wrappers served men, women and youths with coverage varying by age, gender, work and status. Stated only at this general level.
- *Artistic interpretation:* the exact garment cuts, the earth-red dye variant, head-tie styling and figure casting are Design's everyday (non-ceremonial) reads for CH01 background population. NO ceremonial dress, regalia, masks, titled-society insignia or ritual clothing is depicted or authorised by this board — those require their own research + cultural review before any design.
- Modelling guidance: reuse HUMAN_BASE_PROPOSED skeleton and the existing lathe garment pipeline (Guard/Adanna); cloth colours = undyed cream, indigo (shared `cloth_indigo_plain_v1` family), earth-red variant; bare feet/simple sandals; body-type variation is a TD/modelling task beyond the current two hero builds — flagged, not solved here.
