## 2. Environment Kit V1 (compound + construction details) — APPROVED AS PROVISIONAL PRODUCTION REFERENCE
Research basis (documented vs interpretation, kept distinct):
- *Documented, general:* traditional Igbo building in the south-eastern rainforest belt used locally available materials — mud/clay wall construction (wattle-and-daub / puddled clay traditions), steep thatched roofs (palm/raffia thatch) suited to heavy rainfall, family compounds organised around a courtyard with a compound wall and gate, veranda shading, clay pots and woven fibre containers as household storage. These are broad, well-attested material-culture patterns, stated at that general level only.
- *Artistic interpretation (not historical claims):* the specific compound layout, wall curvature, roof proportions and prop placement in these images are Design's composition for the CH01 family house; no specific community, period date, or named building tradition is claimed. No shrine, mask, carved door, or ritual object is depicted — deliberately, as those carry cultural weight this batch has no research mandate to invent.
- *Correction note for modellers:* the detail study's mud plaster reads heavily cracked/derelict; production finish should be smoother maintained clay plaster (the compound view is the plaster authority). Thatched roof layering, eave depth, timber gate and pot/basket forms are approved as shown.
- Status ceiling: CH01 settlement remains **provisional, never canon** (standing rule); this kit guides the existing W1 D7 KIT modules and greybox swap without canonising the settlement.
