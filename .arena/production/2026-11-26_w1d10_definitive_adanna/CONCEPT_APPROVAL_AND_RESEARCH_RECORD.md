## Research notes (summary; no new cultural claims)
- Materials are presented as material studies only: indigo-dyed hand-woven plain cloth, vegetable-tanned leather, aged bronze (dull, patinated — never gold), coral beads. Consistent with the project's existing research-verified palette and the master reference's own cultural note.
- The spiral pendant and the temple bead arrangement are **original fiction** (as recorded on the master reference), not Igbo emblems, and are not presented as historical fact. No idemili, masks, or ṣọ̀ elements introduced. No new culturally significant detail was invented for this set.
- Costume construction read from the approved views: cropped chest wrap (bandeau) with bare midriff; wrapped skirt to below the knee with a folded waistband; simple leather sandals; baldric worn over the wrap, tucking at the skirt waistband, crossing the back.
