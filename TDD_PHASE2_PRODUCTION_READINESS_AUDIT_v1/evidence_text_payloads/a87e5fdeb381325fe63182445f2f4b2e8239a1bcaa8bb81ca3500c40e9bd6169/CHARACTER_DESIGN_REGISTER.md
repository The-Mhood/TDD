## Main characters

### Adanna — master reference APPROVED; 7 further boards REVIEW REQUIRED
- **Role:** protagonist, 25, warrior, daughter of Ezeani.
- **Identity locks:** deep brown skin; thin braids, top half in a high knot; 3 bronze cuffs and 2 coral beads at the left temple; diagonal scar on the left cheekbone; coiled bronze armlet on the left upper arm; spiral bronze pendant (her mother's, fictional design).
- **Build:** athletic, about 7.5 heads.
- **Palette:** indigo, dark leather, aged bronze, coral accent.
- **Weapon:** short leaf-bladed iron sword, worn on the left hip (proposed, OAD-09).
- **Armour canon:** single shoulder guard on the LEFT shoulder (approved).
- **Costume states:** initial appearance, exploration, primary combat armour, upgraded armour, battle-worn, MMUO manifestation.
- **Reference:** `Reference_Control/adanna_master_portrait.png`.

### Ezeani — REVIEW REQUIRED (2 boards)
- **Role:** father, former warrior-king, final boss. Killed Nneka.
- **Identity locks:** early sixties; short grey-white beard; heavy brow; massive ageing frame; old blade scars.
- **Costume:** indigo and oxblood wrapper, coral strands, red cap with eagle feather, ivory bracelet, carved staff. No crown.
- **Outstanding:** warrior-king armour, boss phases, cultural review of regalia.

### Nneka — REVIEW REQUIRED (2 boards)
- **Role:** Adanna's mother, killed by Ezeani. Appears in ancestral memories and manifestations.
- **Identity locks:** about 45; softer, rounder face with Adanna's cheekbones and eyes; no scar; upswept braided crest with ivory-white beads; white and pale blue bead strand; plain white wrapper over her left shoulder; curved chalk lines at the left temple and collarbones.
- **Manifestation:** soft edges, cool moonlit skin, drifting chalk dust. Never glows.
- **Reference:** `Reference_Control/nneka_master_portrait.png`.
- **Outstanding:** living-memory version, expressions.

### Okwu — REVIEW REQUIRED (2 boards)
- **Role:** spirit companion and guide.
- **Identity locks:** about 2.4 m, thin, stooped; charcoal hood with the face withheld; layered charcoal and indigo cloth; floor-length raffia mantle; forked staff with iron bells and a gourd; chalk-pale hands; no visible feet.
- **Reference:** `Reference_Control/okwu_master_portrait.png`.
- **Note:** entirely fictional; kept distinct from real masquerade forms (OAD-04). Needs one more distinguishing feature (OAD-12).

### Amadioha — REVIEW REQUIRED (human form chosen)
- **Role:** ancient divine force of thunder, judgment and destruction. Chapter 5.
- **Form:** human, decided by the art director; consultant approval reported 2026-11-15.
- **Identity locks:** about 5 m; slate-dark skin with pale lightning-figure scars; cloud-white hair and beard; blank white eyes; white wrapper with a red band; fulgurite staff. No armour, crown or weapon.
- **Documented:** thunder and lightning deity associated with justice; colour red; symbol a white ram. The visual design is fiction.
- **Outstanding:** turnaround; possible V002 with more red.

### Idemili — REVIEW REQUIRED (2 boards)
- **Role:** ancient water deity of the river domain. Chapter 4.
- **Identity locks:** about 2.2 m or taller, ageless; always wet; chalked brow and eyelids; heavy coiled hair with cowries trailing into river weed; white and indigo cloth turning to water at the hem; living python across the shoulders. No tail, crown or armour.
- **Documented:** river deity; python is her sacred animal. Appearance is fiction.

### The Hollow King — REVIEW REQUIRED (2 boards)
- **Role:** primary antagonist. Chapter 7.
- **Identity locks:** about 2.6 m with headdress; cracked fired-clay skin; open dark cavity through the torso shedding ash; burial-cloth headdress with corroded bronze rings; ash and faded indigo funerary cloth; bone beads; eyes closed.
- **Concept:** a broken vessel. Entirely fictional.
