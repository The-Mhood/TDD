## OAD-19 — White rams on the Mountain of Thunder (RESOLVED)
Client decision, 2026-11-15: rams are ordinary livestock. No protected status, no special rule; they are treated like goats and other village animals. The white ram remains documented as Amadioha's symbol, but the game makes no gameplay rule from it. The earlier proposal (never enemies or loot) is withdrawn.
