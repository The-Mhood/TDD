# VISUAL STYLE GUIDE — THE DISOBEDIENT DAUGHTER
*Nwanyi Na-anụghị Ihe* · V001 · REVIEW REQUIRED

## Overall art direction
Photoreal, grounded dark fantasy. An original fictional world that draws on researched Igbo language and material culture. The benchmark is the production quality of God of War (2018) and Ragnarök; nothing of their design is borrowed. Every frame should look like a place people have lived in for generations and a body that has done real work.

## Material standards
- **Cloth:** hand-woven, visible slubs and uneven dye. Indigo fades at folds.
- **Leather:** vegetable-tanned, stitched, darkened by sweat and oil.
- **Iron:** forged, hammer-marked, dark patina. Polished only at edges and grips.
- **Bronze:** lost-wax cast, fine rope-like and spiral surface work, brown-green patina. Never mirror-bright.
- **Earth:** red laterite walls, hand-smoothed, repaired in patches.
- **Wood:** hardwood, chip-carved where status allows, smoke-stained indoors.
- **Fibre:** raffia and palm thatch, sun-bleached outside and dark underneath.
- **Clay:** fired terracotta, soot at the base.

## Lighting direction
Natural sources first: sky, sun through canopy, fire, moon. Low-key exposure with detail held in shadow. Supernatural light is small, cool and local, and behaves like fog or chalk dust holding light. No neon, no generic particle bursts.

## Character design principles
1. Identity lives in face, hair, scars and one or two signature objects, not in ornament.
2. Silhouette must read at gameplay distance.
3. Status shows through cloth quality, bearing and a few rank items.
4. Every recurring character has a master reference image that all later generations must use.
5. NPCs vary in age, build, face and trade. No recoloured heroes.

## Environment design principles
1. Buildable: clear ground plane, readable paths, landmarks, scale figures.
2. Wealth, trade and region change the architecture. Not everything is a palace.
3. A location keeps its geography, materials and light across all its boards.
4. Environmental storytelling over decoration: repairs, abandonment, offerings, tool marks.

## Cultural research principles
1. Label everything as **Documented**, **Inspired** or **Fictional** (see `00_MASTER_ART_DIRECTION/Cultural_Reference_Guide/`).
2. Never present an invention as Igbo tradition.
3. Do not use sacred or restricted imagery as decoration. When unsure, leave it out and log it.
4. Regalia, ritual objects and deity depictions need review by an Igbo cultural consultant before approval.

## Visual consistency requirements
- Generate from approved references in `Reference_Control/`.
- Artwork is generated without text. All titles and labels are typeset afterwards, so spelling is exact.
- Each board lists its known issues. A board with unresolved identity errors cannot be approved.
