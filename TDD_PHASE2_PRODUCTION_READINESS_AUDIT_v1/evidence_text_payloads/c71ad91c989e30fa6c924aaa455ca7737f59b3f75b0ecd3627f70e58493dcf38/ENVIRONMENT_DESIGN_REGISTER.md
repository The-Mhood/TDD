**Home settlement baseline (documented references):** compounds of several houses around a courtyard, mud walls, steep palm-thatch roofs, carved doors, painted wall designs.

## Decisions since Batch 1

- **Great Root Mother tree (Chapter 1, approved):** the lone giant tree on the Forbidden Forest horizon is the Great Root Mother's territory. It must be visible from every major clearing and is the navigation landmark for the whole chapter. Every Chapter 1 board should place it or state why it is hidden.
- **Python rule (Chapter 4, approved):** in Idemili's domain pythons are never enemies and never loot. They may appear as ambient wildlife and as signs of her presence. Documented basis: the royal python is sacred to Idemili and is not killed.
