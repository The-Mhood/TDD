# SUPPORTING CAST NAMES — PROPOSAL
Status: REVIEW REQUIRED · 2026-11-15

Brief: old Igbo names that are extinct or barely used.

**How far to trust this list.** The four market-day names are checked against sources. The other nine come from my own knowledge of older Igbo naming, largely names of the kind recorded in literature set in pre-colonial Igboland. I have not verified their meanings, their spelling with tone marks, or how rare they really are today; several survive as surnames. Please have your consultant check each one.

| Role | Name | Meaning as I understand it | Basis | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Village elder | Mgbafo | Girl born on Afo market day | Source-checked | An old woman with an old kind of name |
| Palace servant | Mgborie | Girl born on Orie market day | Source-checked | Plain, unremarkable, easy to overlook |
| Rogue | Mgbokwo | Girl born on Nkwo market day | Source-checked | A common name to hide behind |
| Royal guard | Okorie | Boy born on Orie market day | Source-checked | Ordinary soldier's name; now mostly a surname |
| Warrior companion | Obika | "The heart is greater" | Unverified | Courage and feeling over rank |
| Healer | Ojiugo | A prized pale kola nut | Unverified | Rare and valued |
| Merchant | Akuebue | Built on *aku*, wealth | Unverified | A trader's name |
| Blacksmith | Omenka | Maker of fine things, artisan | Unverified | Names his craft |
| Hunter | Ogbuagu | "Leopard killer" | Unverified; may be a title rather than a given name | His scars suggest he earned it |
| Spiritual specialist | Uchendu | "Thought of life" | Unverified; common as a surname | A man who thinks about life and death |
| Rebel leader | Nwanyibuife | "A woman is also something of worth" | Unverified | Echoes the game's own title |
| Villain's lieutenant | Onwuka | "Death is greater" | Unverified | States the Hollow King's creed |
| Travelling storyteller | Unoka | "Home is supreme" | Unverified | An irony for a man with no home |

**Sources for the market-day names**
- Kalu Ogbaa, "Names and Naming in Chinua Achebe's Novels": https://ans-names.pitt.edu/ans/article/download/930/929
- Igbo calendar: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igbo_calendar

**Deliberately avoided**
- *Mgbeke* (girl born on Eke day): I believe it is used today as a put-down for an unsophisticated woman. Unverified, but not worth the risk.
- Names beginning *Chi-* or *Chukwu-*: overwhelmingly the most common modern pattern, so the opposite of the brief.
