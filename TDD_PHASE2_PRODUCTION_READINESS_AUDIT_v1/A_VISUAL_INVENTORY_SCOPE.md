# A. Visual asset inventory

## A1. What is delivered — and what is not yet complete

The machine-readable catalogue enumerates every currently known physical file, archive member and associated protected/local Phase1 file. It includes exact identities, paths, versions, byte fingerprints where verified, measured raster dimensions/aspects with an explicit fresh or historical exact-payload decode basis, inspection joins and exact Phase1 dispositions where applicable.

**This is not yet a complete interpreted logical-asset inventory.** Exhaustively identifying every depicted costume part, hairstyle, accessory, prop, variation, construction detail, visual duplicate and Bible/image contradiction requires completion of the remaining actual inspections and semantic grouping. Uninspected entries are labelled, not filled with filename-derived design claims.

A missing verified measurement means **not measured**, not zero resolution. Previously measured exact-hash dimensions are retained when ignored source snapshots disappear, explicitly labelled as historical receipts—not a fresh decode or a new content inspection. The mislabelled EXP text is classified by actual listed MIME/payload evidence, not treated as a JPEG because of its extension. Archive CRC and full raster decoding are integrity tests, not visual inspection. Native export bytes are distinguished from the native document's stored metadata size.

## A2. Required per-asset reading of the catalogue

For a source ID, join `A1 → inspection_ledger_rows_1based → A4`; use `A2/A5` to trace identical members and copies. Keep approval on the **specific physical/decision identity**, even when an observation is shared through exact bytes. `C_PER_RASTER_EVIDENCE_MATRIX.csv` records stage-evidence limits; it does not manufacture a technical pass.

The useful distinction is:

- **Reliable visible information:** silhouette, observed colours under the depicted light, visible identity markers, surface appearance, composition and explicit printed qualifications.
- **Reliable written requirements:** only the applicable governing Bible/CD specification, with historical/draft/proposed status retained.
- **Unknown unless separately established:** physical dimensions, hidden surfaces, actual material chemistry, topology/UVs, fastening/attachment, rig/deformation, measured set connectivity, mechanics, animation, culture/period authenticity and production performance.

No universal resolution, texture size or sheet count is imposed. A large board with a small illustration panel or an upscaled source does not provide native close-up detail. A source may be sufficient for visual intent but insufficient as a moving-camera background or a construction specification. Required extra views come from actual shot coverage, not an automatic checklist of every possible sheet.
