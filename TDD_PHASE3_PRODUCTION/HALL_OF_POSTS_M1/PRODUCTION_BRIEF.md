## Precise production requirements

### Approved visual requirements

- Monumental carved hardwood posts; **never stone**.
- Forked post heads, lashings, and great beams that run into depth without a visible end.
- Dense thatched roof structure with rafters and cross members; upper half remains very dark.
- Earthen side walls with bright rectangular exterior openings.
- Long earthen benches for multitudes on both sides.
- Exactly one ordinary small stool for scale.
- Damp-looking earthen central floor; preserve the board's `floor looks wet` carry-forward note rather than correcting it.
- Low-key natural exposure; no flame, lamps, neon, generic particles, wind, day/night invention, figures, animals, regalia, or extra tools.
- Carved bands are deeper than “plain and worn”; first blockout indicates band mass only and does not claim a final motif.

### Technical requirements

- Reusable modules with stable names: post, roof bay, bench, wall/opening, stool, exposure cards.
- Neutral technical units only: `1 unit ≈ 1 m` for camera/layout tests, explicitly not approved story dimensions.
- Separate named geometry nodes for floor, walls, benches, posts, beams, thatch, stool, and render-only exposure cards.
- Portable GLB with PBR material intent plus OBJ geometry interchange.
- Named material palette sampled from the approved board; no calibrated albedo/roughness/normal maps claimed.
- Hero, three-quarter, side, and turntable renders from actual 3D geometry.
- Production manifest with source hash, geometry counts, restrictions, module list, camera proxies, and next milestone.

### Explicit exclusions

- No Adanna or any figure; no CQ-9 blade/sheath work.
- No V013 / Ancestral Establishing / Wall of Houses model interpretation.
- No H3 / Landing / Field of Cloth imagery.
- No six visual-release-only files as production authority.
- No rejected, superseded, failed-correction, external-authority, or limited internal-study asset.
- No claimed final topology, UVs, PBR maps, rig, animation, VFX, compositing, or final edit.
